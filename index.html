About
Gatsby Ventures

Gatsby Ventures LLC is a founder-led e-commerce company registered in New Jersey. We buy branded products in bulk orders directly from brands or their authorized distributors, then sell them through various marketplaces like Amazon, Walmart, and eBay, all while prioritizing authenticity, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

We carry everyday categories, including health and household, beauty and personal care, home and kitchen, and toys and games. We work with suppliers across the United States, and we especially like partnering with companies in the New York, New Jersey and Connecticut area.

Our standards

How we handle your products and your brand across online marketplaces.

  • Your pricing stays intact

    We follow MAP and reseller policies, and we don't race to the bottom on price.

  • Authentic, documented product

    We buy new product from brands and their authorized distributors, and we keep the invoice for every purchase.

  • Listings that look right

    We check titles, images and descriptions on every product we sell, and we report errors to the marketplace to get them corrected.

  • The Value We Bring

    Gatsby Ventures makes a commitment to grow the brand's presence by analyzing product listings. We provide feedback to brands by asking questions such as: Is the product catalog organized? Are sellers destroying the price? Could certain products be bundled? Are product images detailed and to the point?

  • Steady reorders

    We start with an opening order, watch sell-through closely, and reorder on a regular schedule as products sell.

  • Marketplace insight

    We track pricing, reviews and sales on your listings and share what we see, so you know how your products are doing on each marketplace.
